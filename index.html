Our Programs

The Heart of Our Work

Comprehensive dental care and education delivered directly to Belizean communities where it's needed most.

Community Dental Clinics

Free dental treatment provided in community centers and covered pavilions across Belize, staffed by volunteer dentists.

School Screenings

On-site dental checkups and screenings at schools throughout Belize City, San Pedro, and surrounding communities.

Oral Health Education

Fun, hands-on brushing workshops and dental hygiene lessons that give children the tools to care for their teeth.

Community Outreach

Food drives, community meals, and volunteer events that go beyond dental care to support families across Belize.

Ambassador
Our Ambassador

Meet Faith Edgar, Miss World Belize 2025

As our brand ambassador, Faith bridges the stage and the streets — bringing international visibility to Belize's oral health crisis while rolling up her sleeves in community clinics and classrooms.

From dental screenings in San Pedro to brushing workshops in Belize City schools, she's in the community, not above it. Her Beauty with a Purpose project brings dental care to children who have never seen a dentist.

"Every child deserves to smile without pain. That's why I'm here — not on a stage, but in the communities that need us."

Faith Edgar, Miss World Belize 2025
