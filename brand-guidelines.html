Brand Guidelines

Smiles for Belize

Visual identity and brand standards for communicating our mission of dental health access for every child in Belize.

Our Mission

Healthy Teeth,
Happy Life

Smiles for Belize provides free dental care and oral health education to underserved children and communities across Belize. Through volunteer dental clinics, school visits, and community outreach, we're building a future where every child has access to the care they deserve.

Our brand ambassador, Miss World Belize, carries this mission into schools, communities, and international stages — making dental health visible, approachable, and joyful.

Community Outreach

Real moments from dental clinics, school visits, and community events. Candid over posed. Natural light. The warmth of human connection.

Children & Smiles

Kids in school uniforms, laughing, peace signs, playing. Show the joy and energy of the children we serve. Their smiles ARE the brand.

Clinical Care

Dental professionals at work — screenings, cleanings, education. Can use B&W for editorial weight. Always show care and professionalism.

1
Always Authentic

No stock photography. Every image should be from an actual Smiles for Belize event, clinic, or school visit.

2
Natural Light First

Embrace the outdoor settings — covered pavilions, school porches, community halls. The Belizean light is warm and beautiful.

3
Eye-Level with Children

Photograph at the child's height. Never shoot down at them. Show dignity and equality in every frame.

4
B&W for Clinical Depth

Black and white treatment can add editorial gravitas to dental care photos. Use sparingly for impact.

5
Ambassador Integration

Miss World Belize photos bridge glamour and grassroots. Show her in the community, not above it.

6
Show the Setting

Include wide shots of clinic setups, colorful buildings, and Belizean landscapes. Context tells the story.

Voice & Tone
How We Speak
Warm, direct, and hopeful. We talk about real impact in real communities — never abstract or corporate.

Warm & Joyful

Lead with smiles, not statistics. We're a charity built on human connection. Let the warmth of our work come through in every word.

Direct & Honest

Simple, clear language. Say what we do and why it matters. Avoid jargon. If a 10-year-old in Belize City wouldn't understand it, rewrite it.

Hopeful, Not Pitying

We uplift, never pity. Show the strength of the communities we work with. They're partners, not recipients. Focus on possibility.

Community-Centered

The heroes are the volunteers, the dentists, the teachers, and the kids. We're the connector, not the savior. "We" always means the whole community.
